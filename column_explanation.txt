RETAIL DEMAND FORECASTING – COLUMN EXPLANATION

---

1. IDENTIFIER COLUMNS

Store:
Unique identifier for each store. Different stores have different sizes, types, and demand patterns.

Dept:
Department identifier within a store (e.g., Grocery, Electronics, Clothing). Sales are predicted per store and department.

---

2. TIME-RELATED COLUMNS

Date:
Represents the week of sales. The dataset is aggregated at a weekly level.

IsHoliday:
Indicates whether the given week includes a holiday. Holidays significantly impact sales patterns.

---

3. TARGET VARIABLE

Weekly_Sales:
The total sales for a given store and department in a specific week. This is the variable the model predicts.

---

4. ECONOMIC FEATURES

Temperature:
Represents weather conditions. Certain products have seasonal demand (e.g., cold drinks in summer).

Fuel_Price:
Represents fuel cost. Higher fuel prices can reduce store visits and overall spending.

CPI (Consumer Price Index):
Measures inflation. Higher CPI indicates increased prices, reducing purchasing power.

Unemployment:
Represents the unemployment rate. Higher unemployment typically leads to lower consumer spending.

---

5. PROMOTION FEATURES (MARKDOWN)

MarkDown1:
Seasonal promotions.

MarkDown2:
Electronics discounts.

MarkDown3:
Clothing clearance sales.

MarkDown4:
Storewide campaigns.

MarkDown5:
Holiday promotions.

Higher values indicate stronger promotional activity, which directly increases sales.

---

6. STORE CHARACTERISTICS

Type:
Store category:
A = Large scale store
B = Medium scale store
C = Small scale store

Size:
Physical size of the store. Larger stores generally have higher sales capacity.

---

7. TIME ENGINEERED FEATURES

Year:
Captures yearly trends in sales.

Month:
Captures seasonal variations across months.

Week:
Captures weekly trends.

DayOfWeek:
Numerical representation of the day (useful for time structure).

---

8. LAG FEATURES (HISTORICAL MEMORY)

Lag_1:
Sales from the previous week.

Lag_2:
Sales from two weeks ago.

Lag_4:
Sales from four weeks ago (approximately one month).

These features help the model understand recent trends.

---

9. ROLLING FEATURES

Rolling_Mean_4:
Average sales over the last 4 weeks.

Rolling_Std_4:
Standard deviation of sales over the last 4 weeks.

These features capture trend stability and volatility.

---

10. ADVANCED FEATURES (IF USED)

Week_sin, Week_cos:
Cyclical encoding of weeks to capture seasonality.

Total_Markdown:
Sum of all markdown values representing total promotional strength.

Promo_Flag:
Binary indicator (0 or 1) showing whether any promotion is active.

Lag_13:
Sales from 13 weeks ago (approximately 3 months).

Lag_52:
Sales from 52 weeks ago (previous year).

Same_Week_Last_Year:
Sales from the same week in the previous year. Important for year-over-year comparison.

Rolling_Mean_8:
Average sales over the last 8 weeks for longer trend analysis.

---

SUMMARY

The dataset combines:

* Temporal features (date, week, month)
* Economic indicators (CPI, fuel price, unemployment)
* Promotional features (markdowns)
* Historical sales patterns (lags and rolling features)

These collectively help the model accurately predict weekly retail demand.
